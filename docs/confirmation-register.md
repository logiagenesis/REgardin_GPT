# Confirmation register

These items are recorded together so development can proceed without repeated questions. They are launch requirements, not prerequisites to building the design preview.

- Contact: primary monitored email (iCloud versus domain mailbox), phone, WhatsApp permission, hours, privacy contact.
- Business: legal entity, registration, Regard's role, history and any AcB relationship. All omitted until confirmed.
- Services: current scope, exclusions, specialist design/approvals, minimum job size. Decorative flooring, new homes and civil pages omitted.
- Areas: exact service suburbs; no suburb landing pages until substantiated.
- Terms: quote validity, deposits, fees, turnaround, warranties and timelines. No such promises published.
- Operations: supervision, communication, site protection, variations, handover.
- Proof: approved photographs, project names/context, before/after pairings, testimonial permission.
- Brand: approved logo and palette. Current wordmark and palette are proposed design treatments.
- Accounts: authorised Cloudflare invitation, email provider and recipient, existing Google/Meta property access. No passwords requested.
- Privacy: Information Officer, retention, cross-border arrangements and legal approval.
